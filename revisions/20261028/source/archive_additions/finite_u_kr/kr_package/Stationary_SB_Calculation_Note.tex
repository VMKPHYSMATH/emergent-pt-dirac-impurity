\textbf{Status: exploratory numerical result within mean field.}
This calculation is separate from the APS Open Science manuscript revision.
It tests the full stationary radial bath at zero temperature, rather than the
separate Markov slave-boson benchmark. The bath continuum is integrated
directly; the interaction treatment remains a slave-boson approximation.

\section*{1. What the calculation establishes}
An equilibrium finite-$U$ slave-boson saddle can retain a nonlinear continuum
resonance EP. A locally stable example has \emph{mean} impurity occupancy
$n_d=1$ at $U=0.0025$, with the following retuned parameters:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
Quantity & Value in the manuscript's model units\\
\midrule
$\epsilon_d$ & $0.008761698951796$\\
$V_O$ & $0.945864146591530$\\
$\mu$ & $0.006656973796273$\\
$z_{\rm EP}$ & $0.006535134304576-0.002983035811337\ii$\\
$D=\langle n_\uparrow n_\downarrow\rangle$ & $0.228945653167805$\\
Eigenvalues of the local density matrix & $0.4053765225,\ 0.5946234775$\\
Eigenvalues of the quasiparticle weight & $0.9973955023,\ 0.9973955023$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The residuals satisfy $|F|<10^{-17}$, $|F_z|<10^{-12}$ and
$F_{zz}=1.7116469191-0.3435121542\ii\ne0$. The kernel has one vanishing
singular value and one finite singular value, approximately $1.542\times10^{-3}$.
Two poles exchange on a small loop in $(\epsilon_d,V_O)$ after the equilibrium
saddle is re-solved at every loop point. The separation half-winding is $-1/2$.

\textbf{Mean occupancy one is not a local-moment certificate.}
Here the empty and double probabilities are both $0.22895$, and the
single-occupancy probability is only $P_{\rm single}=n_d-2D=0.54211$.
The uncorrelated double occupancy at the same density matrix would be
$\det n=0.24105$. Thus this EP lies in a charge-fluctuating regime with modest
correlation corrections, not an established Kondo or developed local-moment regime.

\textbf{Stronger-$U$ check.}
On the distinct path $\mu=0.012$, $\epsilon_d=\epsilon_d^{(0)}-U/2$,
$V_O=1.21342200961$ and $W=0.1$, the saddle reaches
$n_d=0.994055$, $D=0.00125994$ at $U=0.2$.
It is strongly polarized: the density eigenvalues are $0.001316$ and $0.992739$.
The tested poles are separated; an EP in this occupied regime has not been
established. This gate path is \emph{not} particle-hole symmetry of the asymmetric bath.

\textbf{Additional control choice.}
Holding $V_O$ fixed instead and retuning $\epsilon_d,W,\mu$ also produces
an $n_d=1$ EP branch in the tested weak-to-moderate-$U$ window. In that branch,
the increase of $W$ broadens the resonances and double occupancy remains large.
It does not supply a local-moment EP either. The archive records both choices.

\textbf{Why fixed-parameter splitting is compatible with a retuned EP.}
An interaction-induced change of the saddle generally moves the double-pole
condition. A split pair at one fixed set of controls and a double pole at a
different, retuned set are different statements. Failure of a continuation
solver at larger $U$ is not a proof that no EP exists anywhere in parameter space.

\section*{3. Model and saddle equations}
All frequencies are absolute energies. The state assumed here is the
$T=0$ grand-canonical equilibrium state of the Hermitian stationary
rotating-frame model. This is a preparation assumption; stationarity alone
does not establish thermalization of a laboratory driven system.

The bath parameters are $K=\pi/4$, $\lambda_{\rm SOC}=0.05$,
$\epsilon_O=120$, $\Omega=60$ and, unless varied explicitly, $W=0.1$.
The full physical-sheet hybridization is
\begin{align}
\Delta_b(z)&=W^2\int_0^K\frac{k\,dk}{2\pi}
\left[(z-B(z)-k^2)\Id-\lambda_{\rm SOC}k\sigma_x-A(z)\sigma_z\right]^{-1},\\
B(z)&=\frac{V_O^2(z-\epsilon_O)}{(z-\epsilon_O)^2-\Omega^2}
+\frac{V_O^2\epsilon_O}{\epsilon_O^2-\Omega^2},\qquad
A(z)=-\frac{V_O^2\Omega}{(z-\epsilon_O)^2-\Omega^2}.
\end{align}
The spin-even static subtraction is the same bath-energy convention as the
existing noninteracting certificate. No Markov approximation is made.

A normal single-orbital Kotliar--Ruckenstein/Gutzwiller saddle is parameterized
by local probabilities $(e,p_+,p_-,d)$ summing to one. The symbol $d$ in these
probabilities means $D$, not an impurity annihilation operator. In a variational
natural spin basis, $n_\pm=p_\pm+d$, and
\begin{equation}
r_\pm=\frac{\sqrt{e p_\pm}+\sqrt{d p_\mp}}
{\sqrt{n_\pm(1-n_\pm)}},\qquad
R=\mathcal U_\theta\operatorname{diag}(r_+,r_-)\mathcal U_\theta^\dagger.
\end{equation}
The natural axis is varied, and the real symmetric constraint matrix
$\Lambda=\Lambda_0\Id+\Lambda_x\sigma_x+\Lambda_z\sigma_z$ is independent.
This is a spin-covariant matrix saddle in the normal, real sector of this real
Hamiltonian. No paramagnetic or scalar-$Z$ restriction is imposed.
Transverse complex-spin stability is checked separately.

The functional is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L=\Omega_{\rm qp}(R,\Lambda)
+\Tr[(\epsilon_d\Id-\Lambda)n]+Ud,\qquad
G_f(z)=[z\Id-\Lambda-R\Delta_b(z)R]^{-1}.
\end{equation}
Stationarity imposes the matrix density constraint and the probability and
spin-axis forces. At zero temperature,
\begin{equation}
n_f=\frac{\Id}{2}+\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty
\operatorname{Herm}G_f(\mu+\ii y)\,dy,
\quad \operatorname{Herm}M=\tfrac12(M+M^\dagger).
\end{equation}
The bath-force matrix follows by differentiating the same grand potential.
Independent diagonalization of a Hermitian discretized star checks both the
density and this force. The angular stationarity equation is regularized at
vanishing spin polarization; otherwise polar coordinates can produce spurious
unpolarized stationary points.

For resonance poles, the saddle is held fixed in the spectral variable:
\begin{equation}
K_f^{\rm II}(z)=z\Id-\Lambda-R\Delta_b^{\rm II}(z)R,
\qquad G_{d,\rm coh}^{\rm II}(z)=R[K_f^{\rm II}(z)]^{-1}R.
\end{equation}
The outgoing continuation crosses both open radial channels at the certified
point. The equations $F=\det K_f^{\rm II}=0$ and $F_z=0$ are solved jointly with
the saddle and filling constraint. The saddle is re-solved when physical
controls change, not when differentiating with respect to $z$.

These checks validate a \emph{locally stable mean-field} EP. They do not bound
the error of slave-boson mean field relative to the exact interacting impurity,
prove a global free-energy minimum, or establish a screened Kondo state.
The coherent physical Green function has total weight $RR^\dagger$, not the
full canonical unit spectral sum: incoherent spectral weight is outside this
saddle approximation. Local occupation and double occupancy are obtained
from the constrained local probabilities, not by integrating only this
coherent physical Green function.

At the stronger-$U$ gate-path points the formal saddle poles are

\toprule

\midrule

Their determinant derivatives are nonzero. These are distinct poles, not
double poles. In particular, the large-energy strong-$U$ poles should not be
interpreted as a controlled calculation of Hubbard satellites: the coherent
saddle is a low-energy approximation.

python src/validate.py
python src/plot_results.py
pdflatex Stationary_SB_Calculation_Note.tex
