# Stationary-drive manuscript revision - 28 October 2026

Vinayak M. Kulkarni, *Emergent PT Symmetry and Exceptional Points in a Driven Dirac Impurity*.

Compile `main.tex` first and `supplemental_material.tex` second with pdfLaTeX
and BibTeX, or run `bash build.sh`. The compiled files contain 19 main pages
and 30 supplementary pages. The seven main and five supplementary figures
are retained. `ref.bib` supplies the bibliography; AUX/BBL files support
cross-document references and can be regenerated.

The central result is the noninteracting full-bath stationary-drive resonance
EP. The finite-chain embedding and continuum finite-U KR saddle provide
interaction evidence at their stated levels. The filling-one KR EP is charge
fluctuating. A continuum-converged local-moment EP remains unresolved.

New occupied-bath convergence diagnostics are in SM App. C; the exact
spin-symmetric dimer comparison in App. L is a separate model, not a
replacement for the driven SOC bath. Numerical files for these checks are in
the sibling `checks_20261028/` directory. Existing mean-field checks remain in
`archive_additions/slave_boson_audit_20261024/` and `finite_u_kr/`.

JNCASR is retained with the explicit doctoral-affiliation/independent-work
disclosure. The acknowledgement of Rok Zitko's methodological discussions is
retained. These lines do not imply institutional sponsorship or endorsement.
